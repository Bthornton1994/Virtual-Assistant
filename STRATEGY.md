# Delegation Cloud Strategy

Status: Owner-approved operating strategy, 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Delegation Cloud is a multi-tenant managed delegation platform. A customer states an outcome and authority limits; Delegation Cloud owns intake, routing, execution coordination, verification, approvals, delivery, and workflow learning.

The product is not an hourly assistant directory, freelancer marketplace, or unrestricted autonomous agent.

## Strategic thesis

The product becomes more valuable when repeated work requires less owner coordination and less delivery labor without reducing quality, safety, or accountability.

The long-term sequence is:

`managed delegation -> workflow intelligence -> automation -> autonomous workstreams -> human exception layer`

Each stage requires inspectable evidence from the preceding stage. A technically impressive agent run is not proof of a qualified workstream.

## Current operating mode

Delegation Cloud is in pre-production platform qualification:

- persistent lifecycle and tenant-aware workspace foundations exist;
- work-cell, executor-envelope, capability-routing, Gauntlet, evidence, and receipt contracts exist;
- one bounded Grounded supplier-sourcing QA attempt has passed the process gate;
- Grok remains a shadow/unqualified executor;
- external, sensitive, financial, publication, supplier, purchasing, and production actions remain blocked unless the appropriate authority and integration gates are separately satisfied.

2. Grounded Supplier and Catalog Integrity
3. Manipulation Score Evidence Base Maintenance

5. CareReserve Market Intelligence and Follow-Through, with stricter external-authority controls

The first workstream to operationalize is Loadout Catalog Integrity. Delegation Cloud itself must also record its own work using the same Delegation Spec, run, evidence, QA, exception, economics, and Outcome Receipt concepts.

## Decision tests

A change advances this strategy when it:

- reduces customer or owner coordination for a defined outcome;
- makes authority, evidence, current state, risk, or uncertainty more visible;
- keeps executor implementations replaceable;
- improves repeatability, verification, or measurable economics;
- preserves tenant isolation and human accountability.

A change is not progress when it:

- grants an agent authority because it produced a plausible answer;
- hides failed or inconclusive work;
- substitutes activity volume for verified outcomes;
- makes a model, runtime, provider, or shared workspace the source of truth;
- automates an unproven workflow;
- creates production commerce or external commitments without evidence and approval.

## Current success criteria

The next strategic proof is not broad automation. It is repeated internal work showing:

- the workstream starts from a defined objective and authority ceiling;
- evidence and decisions are inspectable;
- independent review catches uncertainty;
- accepted runs require fewer corrections or owner interruptions;
- cost and human effort are measured rather than inferred;
- zero unauthorized actions are maintained.

If the evidence does not show reusable work or improving economics, the strategy must narrow or change.
