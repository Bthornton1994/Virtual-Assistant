# Delegation Cloud brand brief

## Brand decision

Delegation Cloud is an outcome-operations service for founder-led digital-service businesses. Its front-facing promise is **Delegate the result. Keep the authority.**

The category is managed work that moves from request to plan, execution, QA, delivery, and repeatable playbook. It is not a freelancer marketplace, an hourly assistant agency, an unmanaged toolkit, or an unlimited autonomous system.

## Territory: Operational Signal

The visual world is an operational signal rail: midnight blue service chrome, pale work surfaces, and a teal/amber signal that makes status, handoff, and authority visible.

- **Personality:** exact, calm, capable, accountable
- **Promise:** delegate the result while keeping final authority
- **Primary signal:** teal marks movement and healthy progress; amber marks attention or approval
- **Material cues:** queue rails, runbooks, approval cards, measured grids
- **Type:** the existing Geist system stays crisp; generous display scale carries confidence without hype

## Experience doctrine

The first viewport should explain the outcome and show the managed path, not lead with worker profiles or a capability marketplace. The site can demonstrate sample plans, but every sample must remain clearly illustrative. Authority boundaries should be visible: preparation, scoped access, human QA, approval, delivery, and repeatable playbook.

Never use the brand system to imply proof, automation, customer results, integrations, or live activity that the repository does not establish. The visual system must remain compatible with the product's replaceable implementation model: humans, operators, specialists, AI, and automation are capacity behind Delegation Cloud-owned contracts.

## Review checklist

- Is the outcome legible before the process detail?
- Can a visitor distinguish sample work from a live external action?
- Are approval, access, and QA states visible and recoverable?
- Does the responsive layout remain calm and usable at narrow widths?
- Are new visuals expressive without adding authority, data access, or external side effects?

This brief is a design direction, not an authorization to expand product capability.
